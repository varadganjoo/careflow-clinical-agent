# ADA Standards of Care in Diabetes (2025/2026)

## Diagnostic & Glycemic Targets
- **Normal**: HbA1c < 5.7%
- **Prediabetes**: HbA1c 5.7% – 6.4%
- **Diabetes**: HbA1c >= 6.5%
- **General Glycemic Target**: HbA1c < 7.0% (individualized to < 6.5% for young/healthy or < 8.0% for frail elderly).

## Pharmacotherapy Decision Tree
1. **First-line**: Metformin + comprehensive lifestyle modification.
2. **Comorbid CKD (eGFR < 60 or UACR >= 30 mg/g)**:
   - Add SGLT2 inhibitor (Empagliflozin, Dapagliflozin) for nephroprotection.
   - If SGLT2i not tolerated, add GLP-1 RA.
3. **Comorbid ASCVD or High Cardiovascular Risk**:
   - Prioritize GLP-1 RA with proven CVD benefit (Semaglutide, Dulaglutide) or SGLT2i.

## Safety Invariants
- **Metformin contraindication**: Do NOT initiate or maintain Metformin if eGFR < 30 mL/min/1.73m2.
- Dose reduction required if eGFR is 30–44 mL/min (max 1000 mg/day).
